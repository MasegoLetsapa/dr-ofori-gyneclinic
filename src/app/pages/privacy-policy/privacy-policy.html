PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Dr. Ofori Gyne Clinic handles information submitted through this website.

1. Introduction

Dr. Ofori Gyne Clinic respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, why it is collected and how it may be used.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide.

Appointment requests

  • • First name and last name
  • • Email address
  • • Phone number
  • • Requested service
  • • Preferred appointment date and time
  • • Practice location
  • • Optional message

Contact enquiries

  • • First name and last name
  • • Email address
  • • Phone number, where provided
  • • Subject of the enquiry
  • • Message content

3. Website and Usage Information

The website may also process technical and usage-related information associated with interactions with our website, including visitor identifiers, session information and traffic-source information such as the source, medium and campaign through which a visitor reached the website.

4. How We Use Information

  • • To respond to enquiries.
  • • To process appointment requests.
  • • To contact you regarding an appointment request.
  • • To provide information about clinic services.
  • • To maintain and improve our website.
  • • To understand website traffic and usage.
  • • To maintain appropriate administrative records.

5. Medical Information

The website contact form is intended for general enquiries and clinic-related communication. Please do not submit highly sensitive medical information through the general contact form.

Information submitted through an appointment request should be limited to information reasonably necessary to request and arrange an appointment.

6. Appointment Requests

Submitting an appointment request through the website does not automatically mean that an appointment has been confirmed. The clinic team will review availability and contact you to confirm the appointment.

7. Sharing of Information

Personal information may be accessed by authorised persons who require it to respond to enquiries, process appointment requests, administer the clinic website or provide relevant technical services.

Information may also be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies, visitor identifiers, session information and similar technologies to support website functionality, understand usage and measure traffic.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information held about you, including rights to request access to or correction of information and to raise concerns about how information is handled.

Requests relating to personal information can be directed to the clinic using the contact details provided on this website.

11. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, for legitimate administrative purposes, or where retention is required by applicable law.

12. Third-Party Services

The website may rely on third-party technology and service providers to support hosting, analytics, communication, security or other website functionality. Where applicable, those providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology or applicable legal requirements.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact Dr. Ofori Gyne Clinic through the contact details provided on this website.

This Privacy Policy is provided for the website and should be reviewed and approved by the clinic before publication.
